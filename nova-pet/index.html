Nova Pet

Your own alien friend

Coming to Google Play on 8 December

A cozy virtual alien pet game for Android, made for children aged 6 to 15 by one developer in Romania. Care for a cute virtual alien pet, play 10 mini-games and fix its little ship.

For grown-ups

  • The alien can't die: after a weekend away, it's simply happy to see you again.
  • It plays offline: the pet, the mini-games and the story need no Wi-Fi or data.
  • Free to download. From the second day, a short video now and then between games, at most 5 a day. One purchase, No ads, removes every video.
  • No accounts and no chat. The ads come from Google AdMob with its child settings: no personalised ads and no advertising ID.
  • Purchases, Reset Game and the privacy policy link sit behind a grown-up check.
