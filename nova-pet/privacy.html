Nova Pet

Privacy policy

For the Android game Nova Pet (Nova Pet: Virtual Alien Pet on Google Play), made by Nova Games Devs. Last updated: 30 October 2026.

In short

  • We, the people who make Nova Pet, receive no personal information from the game or from children. The only thing that can reach us is an email a grown-up chooses to send us (Report a problem, below).
  • Everything the game remembers stays in its save on the phone.
  • Nova Pet shows short ads from Google AdMob, set up for children: no personalised ads and no advertising ID. To show them, Google's ad code receives some technical data, listed below.
  • Grown-ups can buy optional extras through Google Play, behind a grown-up check. We never see any payment details.
  • No accounts, no chat, no analytics of our own and no other ad companies.

Ads

Ads are how Nova Pet stays free to download. They come from Google AdMob, which is on Google Play's list of ad services that meet its rules for children's apps.

  • When: from the second day of play, a short video now and then between two games, at most 5 a day. Never when the game opens, and never while the player looks after the alien, decorates or reads the story.
  • Bonus videos play only when the player taps for one, for a double reward or one more go, at most 6 a day.
  • Settings on every ad request, for every player: marked as coming from a child, so Google shows no personalised ads and no ads based on what someone did in other apps or websites. No advertising ID: the game doesn't have Android's permission to read it. Only ads rated for all ages. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent tool is set for players under the age of consent, so it never asks the child to agree to anything.

Google's ad code then receives what Google lists for it:

  • The phone's internet address (IP address), which can show roughly where the phone is, such as the town.
  • How ads and the game are used: the game starting, taps, and videos shown and watched.
  • How well the game and the ad code run, such as start-up time and freezes.
  • Device identifiers: the app set ID (an ID that Android shares only between apps from the same developer on the phone) and, where they apply, identifiers related to Google accounts signed in on the phone, as Google describes them. Not the advertising ID.

Google uses this to show the ads, count them and stop fraud. It travels encrypted, and Google keeps and deletes it under its own policies: how Google uses information from apps that use its services, how long Google keeps data and the Google Privacy Policy.

We only see totals in our AdMob account, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned. Nothing there tells us who played.

If an ad is tapped, it may open Google Play or a web page. After No ads (or the Supporter pack) is bought, the game stops loading ads, and the bonuses come without a video.

Purchases

  • Grown-ups can buy optional extras: No ads, and packs of outfits, room decorations, Rover paints and photo frames. Coins and crystals are never sold, and nothing random is sold.
  • Every purchase starts behind the grown-up check, and Google Play takes the payment. We never see the buyer's name, card or other payment details: Google Play collects them itself.
  • Google Play tells the game, on the phone, which extras the phone's Google account owns, so they unlock and come back after a reinstall. The game doesn't send this anywhere.
  • Refunds go through Google Play: Google Play's refund policies.

Ratings

Now and then, Google Play's own rating card may appear in the game: after a purchase, or after a happy moment such as the alien growing up. That's never in the first days of play, and at most twice apart from purchases. The card belongs to Google: the game isn't told whether a rating was given. If you rate Nova Pet from inside the game, the rating goes to Google Play, not to us, under Google's terms. The grown-up page in Settings also has Rate Nova Pet on Google Play, which opens the store page.

Photos

Photo mode takes pictures of the game itself: the alien and its room, drawn by the game. It never uses the camera. The pictures go into the phone's gallery, in an album called Nova Pet.

On Android 9 and older, the game asks for permission to save to the phone's storage the first time a photo is taken, and uses it only for that. Newer Android versions need no permission. The game never looks at the other pictures on the phone.

In the gallery, Nova Pet's pictures are like any other picture: you can delete them there, and a photo backup app treats them like your other pictures.

Phone features the game uses

  • Light sensor. Feeding the alien uses the phone's light sensor, which gives just one number: how bright the room is. The game reads it while the player feeds the alien and doesn't save or send it. On phones without the sensor, the player holds a finger on the alien instead.
  • Notifications. Gentle reminders when the alien really needs something while the game is closed, only after the player says yes (the game asks on the second day) or turns them on in Settings. Weekdays 15:00–20:00 and weekends 10:00–20:00, never at night, at most 2 a day. The game plans them on the phone and Android shows them; no server is involved. Turn them off in Settings or in Android's notification settings.
  • Vibration. Short buzzes in the games and when the alien reacts. It can be turned off in Settings.
  • Internet. Used only by Google's ad code and Google Play, to load ads and handle purchases. The game's own data is never sent. Without internet, everything plays as usual, just without videos.
  • Not used at all: the camera, the microphone, GPS location, contacts and the advertising ID.

Report a problem

In Settings, behind the grown-up check, the grown-up page has Report a problem. It opens your own email app with a message to us. The message has room for your own words, then the game's version, the phone's Android version, whether the game's last save worked, and the notes about the last errors. You see all of it before anything is sent, and you can change or delete any of it. Nothing is sent unless you press Send in your email app.

If you send it, we receive it like any other email, with your email address. We use it only to fix the game and to answer you, and delete it once that's done: at the latest 90 days after our last reply. Our address, novagamesdevs@gmail.com, is a Gmail address, so Google carries and stores the email for us under the Google Privacy Policy.

Android's own backup

If backup is turned on for the phone, Android may copy the game's save to the Google account used on that phone, as it does for other apps. That way a child who gets a new phone keeps their alien. This backup is a feature of Android, run by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We can't see it or reach it. You can turn it off in the phone's backup settings. Photos in the gallery aren't part of the game's backup.

Other companies

Only Google, in the two ways above: AdMob for the ads and Google Play for purchases. No other ad, analytics or tracking companies. The game is built with open-source tools (Kivy, python-for-android and others), which run only on the phone and collect nothing.

For players in Europe and the UK: the ads rest on our legitimate interest in keeping the game free to download (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR). Because the players are children, their interests count for more, so the ads are set up to protect them: no personalised ads, no profiling, no advertising ID, no other ad companies, no ads on the first day and at most 5 videos a day. We don't ask for consent, because a child can't give it for themselves and the game has no reliable way to check a parent's. Purchases rest on carrying out what the grown-up asked to buy (Article 6(1)(b)). You can object at any time: without an internet connection, the game plays as usual, shows no ads, and Google's ad code sends nothing. You can ask us anything (below), and you can complain to your country's data protection authority.

Children

Nova Pet is made for children aged 6 to 15. The game asks for an age group (7–10, 11–14 or 15+) only to set how hard some games and puzzles are. That choice doesn't change the ads, the data or the purchases: every player is treated as a child, whatever they pick, so the child settings above apply to everyone. We don't collect personal information from children. In the game, the things meant for grown-ups sit behind a grown-up check: the grown-up shop (also when a child taps a quiet From the grown-up shop row, which shows no price), Reset Game, and the grown-up page with the link to this page, the store page and Report a problem. Apart from those, only a tapped ad can lead out of the game.

How long things are kept

  • On the phone: the save stays until you reset the game or uninstall it. Only the last 5 error notes are kept; each new one replaces the oldest. The day's ad counts start again each day, and the week's line covers only the last 7 days.
  • Emails sent with Report a problem: kept until the problem is fixed and you have an answer, then deleted, at the latest 90 days after our last reply.
  • Google: what Google's ad code and Google Play receive is kept under Google's own rules: how long Google keeps data.
  • Android's backup, if it's on: kept with the Google account's phone backups, where you can delete it.

Deleting everything

  • In the game: Settings → Reset Game (after the grown-up check) deletes the save, ad counters and error notes included, and starts a new game. It keeps one thing: when Google's rating card was last asked for, so a new game doesn't bring the card sooner. Bought extras stay with the Google account and come back with Restore in the grown-up shop.
  • Or uninstall Nova Pet. Android deletes its save with it.
  • Pictures in the gallery stay until you delete them there.
  • A copy made by Android's backup is kept with the Google account's phone backups, where you can delete it.
  • What Google's ad code received never reaches us, so we can't look it up or delete it. Google's page above explains how long it keeps ad data.

If this changes

If a later version of Nova Pet changes any of this, for example with a new kind of notification, this page changes before that version comes out, and the date at the top changes with it.
